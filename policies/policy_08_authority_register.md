# Policy Authority Register

**Document ID:** POL-AUTH-REG-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CURRENT  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Expiry Date:** None (open-ended until superseded)  
**Scope:** All departments — all employees and system administrators  
**Authorised by:** Northstar Services People Operations and Legal  

---

## AUTH-REG-P1 — Purpose

This register defines the rules of precedence that govern which policy document applies when two or more documents address the same topic for the same date and department. These rules are used by the Northstar Policy Knowledge Assistant to resolve retrieval results before generating an answer.

## AUTH-REG-P2 — Current Policy Outranks Archived Policy

For the same topic and the same effective date, an applicable current policy outranks an archived policy. If a current policy and an archived policy both address the same question, the current policy is authoritative and the archived policy must not be cited as the applicable rule. This applies in particular to the transition from the 2025 Learning Leave Policy (archived) to the 2026 Learning Leave Policy (current).

## AUTH-REG-P3 — Department Exception Outranks General Policy

A current, explicit department exception outranks the general policy for employees in that department. Where a department exception has been issued and is in effect, it governs the allowance for that department's employees, overriding the general allowance. The exception does not affect employees in other departments, who continue to follow the general policy. As of 1 March 2026, Support Operations operates under a remote-work exception (POL-REMOTE-SOPEX-2026).

## AUTH-REG-P4 — FAQ Does Not Override Policy

A Frequently Asked Questions document explains and illustrates the current policy through examples. It does not create new entitlements, raise or lower caps or limits, waive conditions, or override any provision of an authoritative policy document. Where a FAQ example appears to suggest a different outcome from the current policy, the policy governs.

## AUTH-REG-P5 — Conflict Disclosure Obligation

If two applicable, authoritative, current documents with the same scope genuinely conflict on a material point, the conflict must be disclosed rather than resolved by guessing. The assistant must flag the conflict and advise the employee to seek clarification from their manager or People Operations. This register does not authorise any party to invent a resolution to an unresolved policy conflict.

---

*This authority register does not itself establish entitlements. It governs document precedence only. Entitlements are defined in the individual policy documents it references.*
