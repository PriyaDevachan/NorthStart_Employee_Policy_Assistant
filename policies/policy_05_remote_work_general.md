# Remote Work Policy — Current (General)

**Document ID:** POL-REMOTE-CURR-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CURRENT  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Expiry Date:** None (open-ended until superseded)  
**Scope:** All departments — eligible employees (see department exceptions)  
**Authorised by:** Northstar Services People Operations  

---

## REMOTE-CURR-P1 — General Eligibility

Eligible employees at Northstar Services may work remotely subject to the conditions set out in this policy. Eligibility is determined by role, department, and completion of any required onboarding obligations (see the Onboarding Policy). Department-level exceptions may reduce the remote-work allowance; where a department exception exists, it takes precedence over this general policy for employees in that department.

## REMOTE-CURR-P2 — General Remote-Work Allowance

Effective 1 January 2026, eligible employees may work remotely up to **two days per week**. The remaining working days must be spent at the designated Northstar Services office location, unless otherwise approved. This allowance applies by default to all eligible employees not covered by a department-specific exception.

## REMOTE-CURR-P3 — Manager Approval Requirement

Manager approval is required before an employee begins working remotely. Employees must obtain approval for their regular remote-work schedule and must seek additional approval for any change to that schedule. Working remotely without manager approval is not permitted under this policy.

## REMOTE-CURR-P4 — Department Exceptions

Certain departments operate under remote-work exceptions that adjust the general allowance stated in REMOTE-CURR-P2. Where a current, explicit department exception exists, that exception governs the remote-work allowance for employees in that department. Employees should confirm with their manager whether a department exception applies to their role. As of 1 March 2026, Support Operations operates under a separate exception (see POL-REMOTE-SOPEX-2026).

## REMOTE-CURR-P5 — Onboarding Prerequisite

New employees must complete mandatory security training, as specified in the Onboarding Policy (POL-ONBOARD-CURR-2026), before they are eligible to receive manager approval for remote work. This policy does not specify a time limit for completing security training; refer to the Onboarding Policy for details.

---

*This policy does not address learning leave, travel expenses, parental leave, tuition reimbursement, relocation allowances, or equipment reimbursement for personal purchases.*
