# Learning Leave Policy — Current

**Document ID:** POL-LEARN-CURR-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CURRENT  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Expiry Date:** None (open-ended until superseded)  
**Scope:** All departments — full-time employees  
**Authorised by:** Northstar Services People Operations  

---

## LEARN-CURR-P1 — Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed at least six months of continuous service with Northstar Services are eligible to request learning leave under this policy. Part-time, contract, and casual employees are not covered by this policy.

## LEARN-CURR-P2 — Annual Entitlement

Eligible employees may request up to **five working days** of learning leave per calendar year for approved learning and professional development activities. The five-day entitlement resets on 1 January each calendar year.

## LEARN-CURR-P3 — Manager Approval Requirement

Manager approval is required before an employee books or commences any learning leave. Requests must be submitted with sufficient advance notice to allow the manager to assess operational impact. Learning leave taken without prior manager approval will not be recognised under this policy.

## LEARN-CURR-P4 — No Carry-Over

Unused learning leave days do not carry over to the following calendar year. Any days not used by 31 December of the applicable year are forfeited. No cash equivalent is payable for unused learning leave.

## LEARN-CURR-P5 — Relationship to Other Policies

This policy supersedes the 2025 Learning Leave Policy (archived), which applied during calendar year 2025 only. For questions about remote work, travel expenses, or equipment, refer to the relevant current policy documents.

---

*This document contains no entitlements regarding parental leave, tuition reimbursement, relocation allowances, or laptop repair timelines. Those topics are not addressed in this policy.*
