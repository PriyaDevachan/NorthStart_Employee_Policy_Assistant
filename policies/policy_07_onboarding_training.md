# Onboarding Policy — Current

**Document ID:** POL-ONBOARD-CURR-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CURRENT  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Expiry Date:** None (open-ended until superseded)  
**Scope:** All departments — new employees  
**Authorised by:** Northstar Services People Operations and IT Security  

---

## ONBOARD-CURR-P1 — Purpose

This policy sets out the mandatory onboarding requirements that all new Northstar Services employees must complete before they are eligible for certain workplace entitlements. Completion of onboarding requirements is tracked by People Operations and IT.

## ONBOARD-CURR-P2 — Mandatory Security Training Requirement

All new employees must complete mandatory security awareness training before they are eligible to receive manager approval for remote work. This training covers data handling, acceptable use of company systems, and information security obligations. Remote-work approval will not be granted until IT confirms that the security training requirement has been fulfilled.

## ONBOARD-CURR-P3 — No Fixed Completion Deadline Stated in This Document

This policy does not specify a fixed number of days within which the security training must be completed. Employees are expected to complete the training promptly after commencement. Managers are responsible for ensuring new team members are aware of this requirement.

## ONBOARD-CURR-P4 — Other Onboarding Requirements

Additional onboarding steps — such as system access provisioning, mandatory induction sessions, and payroll setup — may apply depending on the employee's role and department. These requirements are communicated by People Operations at the time of commencement and are outside the scope of this policy.

## ONBOARD-CURR-P5 — Relationship to Remote Work Policy

The requirement in ONBOARD-CURR-P2 operates as a prerequisite to the Remote Work Policy (POL-REMOTE-CURR-2026). An employee who has not completed mandatory security training is not eligible for remote-work approval regardless of their department's general allowance. No other entitlement or benefit is conditioned on onboarding completion within this document.

---

*This policy does not address learning leave, travel expenses, equipment replacement, parental leave, tuition reimbursement, or relocation. Those topics are covered in separate policies.*
