# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy — Current

**Document ID:** POL-TRAVEL-CURR-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CURRENT  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Expiry Date:** None (open-ended until superseded)  
**Scope:** All departments — all employees travelling on approved business  
**Authorised by:** Northstar Services Finance and Operations  

---

## TRAVEL-CURR-P1 — Approved Business Travel Requirement

Meal reimbursement under this policy applies only to approved business travel. Travel must be authorised by an employee's manager before departure. Expenses incurred on travel that has not been approved in advance are not eligible for reimbursement under this policy.

## TRAVEL-CURR-P2 — Meal Reimbursement Cap

Meal costs incurred during approved business travel are reimbursable up to a maximum of **CAD 35 per day**. This cap applies to the total of all meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) within a single calendar day of travel. Amounts above the daily cap are not reimbursable and are the employee's personal responsibility.

## TRAVEL-CURR-P3 — Receipt Submission Requirement

A valid receipt must be submitted for each claimed meal expense. Receipts must be submitted to the employee's manager and the Finance team within **30 calendar days** of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after the 30-day window will not be processed unless an exception is approved in writing by Finance.

## TRAVEL-CURR-P4 — Alcohol Exclusion

Alcoholic beverages are excluded from reimbursement under this policy regardless of whether they are consumed during approved business travel. Alcohol costs must not be included in meal expense claims. Any claim that includes alcohol costs will be reduced or rejected.

## TRAVEL-CURR-P5 — Other Expense Categories

This document covers meal reimbursement only. Accommodation, ground transport, and airfare are governed by separate authorisations and are not addressed here. This policy does not establish any entitlement regarding parental leave, learning leave, remote work, or equipment.
