# Remote Work Policy — Support Operations Department Exception

**Document ID:** POL-REMOTE-SOPEX-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CURRENT (Department Exception)  
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2026  
**Expiry Date:** None (open-ended until superseded)  
**Scope:** Support Operations department only  
**Authorised by:** Northstar Services People Operations and Support Operations Leadership  

> **Scope Notice:** This exception applies exclusively to employees in the **Support Operations** department. Employees in all other departments are governed by the general Remote Work Policy (POL-REMOTE-CURR-2026), which allows up to two days per week. This exception does not apply to, and does not affect, any other department.

---

## REMOTE-SOPEX-P1 — Purpose and Scope

This document records the department-level exception to the general Remote Work Policy (POL-REMOTE-CURR-2026) that applies to the Support Operations department. It takes effect on 1 March 2026. Under the authority rules set out in POL-AUTH-REG-2026, this current, explicit department exception outranks the general remote-work allowance for Support Operations employees.

## REMOTE-SOPEX-P2 — Support Operations Remote-Work Allowance

Effective 1 March 2026, Support Operations staff may work remotely up to **one day per week**. This replaces the general allowance of two days per week for Support Operations employees only. The reduced allowance reflects the operational requirements of the Support Operations function, which requires greater in-office presence to maintain service continuity.

## REMOTE-SOPEX-P3 — Manager Approval Requirement

Manager approval is still required for Support Operations employees wishing to work remotely, consistent with the general Remote Work Policy. The employee's manager must approve the specific remote-work day before the arrangement commences. Working remotely without manager approval is not permitted.

## REMOTE-SOPEX-P4 — Other Departments Not Affected

Employees in departments other than Support Operations are not affected by this exception. Those employees continue to follow the general Remote Work Policy (POL-REMOTE-CURR-2026), which allows up to two days per week subject to manager approval and other conditions in that policy.

## REMOTE-SOPEX-P5 — Relationship to Onboarding Requirements

The onboarding prerequisite in POL-ONBOARD-CURR-2026 (mandatory security training before remote-work approval) continues to apply to Support Operations employees. This exception does not waive any onboarding requirement.

---

*This document does not address learning leave, travel expenses, equipment, parental leave, tuition reimbursement, or relocation. It applies only to remote-work allowance for Support Operations employees from 1 March 2026 onward.*
