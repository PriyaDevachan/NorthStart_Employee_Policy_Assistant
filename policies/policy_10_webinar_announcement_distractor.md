## DISTRACT-P1 — Upcoming Webinar: Introduction to Agile Retrospectives

The Northstar Services Learning and Events Team is pleased to announce an upcoming internal webinar, "Introduction to Agile Retrospectives," scheduled for Thursday, 26 March 2026, from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM local time. The session will be hosted on the company's virtual meeting platform.

## DISTRACT-P2 — Webinar Content Overview

The webinar will cover the purpose of sprint retrospectives, commonly used formats such as Start-Stop-Continue and the 4Ls, and practical facilitation tips for team leads. The session is intended for team leads, project coordinators, and anyone interested in improving team collaboration practices. No prior experience with Agile methodology is required.

## DISTRACT-P3 — Registration and Attendance

Participation in this webinar does not constitute learning leave under any policy. If an employee wishes to attend during working hours, they should discuss scheduling with their manager as they would for any internal training activity.
