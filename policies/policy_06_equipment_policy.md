# Equipment Policy — Current

**Document ID:** POL-EQUIP-CURR-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** CURRENT  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026  
**Expiry Date:** None (open-ended until superseded)  
**Scope:** All departments — all employees  
**Authorised by:** Northstar Services IT and Operations  

---

## EQUIP-CURR-P1 — Standard Work Laptop Provision

Northstar Services supplies each employee with a standard work laptop upon commencement of employment. The specification of the standard laptop is determined by IT and may vary by role. The laptop remains the property of Northstar Services at all times.

## EQUIP-CURR-P2 — Laptop Replacement Schedule

Laptop replacement is considered after **36 months** of use from the date the device was issued. At the 36-month point, IT will assess the device's condition and determine whether replacement is warranted. Replacement before 36 months may be considered if IT confirms in writing that a hardware failure has occurred and the device cannot be repaired within a reasonable timeframe.

## EQUIP-CURR-P3 — Hardware Failure Process

If an employee believes their device has experienced a hardware failure, they must report the issue to IT via the standard service desk process. IT will assess the fault. Only a written confirmation of hardware failure from IT triggers eligibility for early replacement. This policy does not guarantee any specific repair or replacement timeline.

## EQUIP-CURR-P4 — Personal Purchases Not Reimbursed

Personal equipment purchases — including laptops, monitors, peripherals, or accessories bought by an employee using personal funds — are not reimbursed under this policy. Employees must not purchase equipment with the expectation of reimbursement unless a separate, pre-approved arrangement has been made in writing with their manager and Finance.

## EQUIP-CURR-P5 — Scope Limitation

This policy covers only the standard work laptop. It does not address mobile phones, printers, standing desks, or other peripherals unless IT explicitly designates such items as standard equipment for a given role. This policy establishes no entitlement regarding parental leave, learning leave, remote work, travel expenses, or tuition reimbursement.

---

*Note: This policy does not guarantee a specific laptop repair time. Questions about guaranteed repair timelines are not established in this document.*
