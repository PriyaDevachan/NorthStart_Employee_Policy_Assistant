# Travel Expense Reimbursement — Frequently Asked Questions

> **Note:** This FAQ provides illustrative examples to help employees understand the Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-TRAVEL-CURR-2026). The examples below do not alter, expand, or override any condition, limit, or exclusion stated in that policy. Where any FAQ answer appears to conflict with the current policy, the policy governs.

---

## TRAVEL-FAQ-P1 — What is the daily meal limit?

The current meal reimbursement cap is CAD 35 per calendar day of approved business travel. This total covers all meals consumed in that day. For example, if an employee spends CAD 12 on breakfast, CAD 15 on lunch, and CAD 10 on dinner, the total is CAD 37; the employee may claim CAD 35 and absorbs the remaining CAD 2 personally.

## TRAVEL-FAQ-P2 — Can I include a glass of wine with my business dinner?

No. Alcoholic beverages are excluded from reimbursement regardless of the dining context. If a meal receipt includes alcohol, only the non-alcoholic portion (up to the daily cap) is eligible. The employee must separate the alcohol cost before submitting the claim.

## TRAVEL-FAQ-P3 — How soon must I submit my receipts?

Receipts must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the date the expense occurred. For example, a meal purchased on 10 March 2026 must have its receipt submitted no later than 9 April 2026. Late submissions require written approval from Finance and are not guaranteed to be accepted.

## TRAVEL-FAQ-P4 — What if I forget a receipt?

This FAQ does not grant any exception to the receipt requirement. The current policy requires a valid receipt for each claimed expense. Employees who lose a receipt should contact Finance as soon as possible to understand what documentation may be accepted in lieu. This FAQ cannot authorise reimbursement without documentation.

## TRAVEL-FAQ-P5 — Does this FAQ change the policy?

No. This document explains the current policy through examples. It does not create new entitlements, raise or lower the CAD 35 cap, extend the 30-day submission window, or remove the approval or receipt conditions. All reimbursement decisions are made in accordance with POL-TRAVEL-CURR-2026.
