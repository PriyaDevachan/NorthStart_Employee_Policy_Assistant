**Document ID:** POL-LEARN-ARCH-2025

**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Expiry Date:** 31 December 2025  
**Scope:** All departments — eligible employees  
**Authorised by:** Northstar Services People Operations  

> ⚠️ **ARCHIVED DOCUMENT:** This policy applied during calendar year 2025 only and is no longer in effect. For the current learning leave entitlement, refer to the Learning Leave Policy (Current), effective 1 January 2026.

---

## LEARN-ARCH-P1 — 2025 Scope and Status

This document records the learning leave entitlement that was in effect during calendar year 2025. It is retained for reference purposes only. It does not apply to any leave requests made or taken in 2026 or later. The current policy (effective 1 January 2026) governs all requests from that date forward.

## LEARN-ARCH-P2 — 2025 Annual Entitlement

During calendar year 2025, eligible employees could request up to **three working days** of learning leave per calendar year for approved learning and professional development activities.

## LEARN-ARCH-P3 — 2025 Approval Requirement

Manager approval was required before an employee booked or commenced any learning leave during 2025. Requests taken without prior manager approval were not recognised under the 2025 policy.

## LEARN-ARCH-P4 — Transition Note

Employees who did not use their 2025 learning leave entitlement before 31 December 2025 forfeited those days. The 2025 entitlement of three days is not transferable to the 2026 entitlement of five days.
